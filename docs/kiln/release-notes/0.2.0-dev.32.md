# Kiln 0.2.0-dev.32 beta 1

A test build on the beta channel, for anyone who wants to try it: 0.2.0-dev.31.24 plus the first round of dev.32. Mods can now add pets and taming, give villagers their own dialogue, and edit creatures, buffs, spells, stations, skills, world rules, limits and loot through twelve new registries.

## Players and server admins

- **How to get it.** In the launcher, Settings > Updates > Channel: pick Beta, and the launcher offers this build. Anyone may join the beta. To go back, set Channel to the release again: the launcher offers the next release when it is out. A world played on a test build may not load the same way on the release, so keep a copy of a world you care about, and a server and its players must run the same build to play together.
- **Mods made for this beta need it.** A mod built with this beta's dev kit asks for mod API 0.2.27, and 0.2.0-dev.31.24 or older refuses to load it. Mods made for 0.2.0-dev.31.x keep working on this beta.
- **Pets, for mods that add them.** A pet mod can now give players pets that are saved with their owner's character, follow them, stay where they are told, fight beside them, go down instead of dying and get revived. The mod decides how its pets behave: what they may attack, what a revive costs, how many pets a player may have. A pet walks beside its owner where Kiln has measured that creature's body; otherwise it catches up with its owner on safe ground. Without a pet mod nothing changes, and Kiln writes nothing to your saves.
- **Taming, for mods that add it.** A mod can let players tame creatures step by step (feed it, weaken it, use a tool on it) and decides what each step costs and whether a failed step is paid back. Mods can also build on the game's own taming of wild animals. If a player leaves the game in the middle of a tame whose mod holds the paid items until the end, those items are not given back yet in this beta.
- **NPC dialogue for mods.** Building on 0.2.0-dev.31.16's speech bubbles: a mod's replaced greeting now picks a new line each time a villager greets you instead of repeating one, and mods can set farewells, the line the talk menu opens with (different for each player if the mod wants), and a talk page of their own topics, also for villagers that have none. Players on 0.2.0-dev.31.16 to 31.24 see the greetings as before; players without Kiln see the game's own lines. Thanks to Marshall (Ryrin), whose Living Larder asked for it.
- **Mods can change much more of the game's data, and keep working after game updates.** Twelve new registries: creatures, enemy attacks, perks, skill costs, crafting stations, buff durations and stacking, spell and ability timing, world rules (no-build zones, play area, difficulty settings), limits (more than 16 Flame Altars, a gifting range past 50 m) and loot (the world-wide rarity weights and every random loot table). Mods edit them by Kiln's names, so after a game update only Kiln needs an update. They are new and experimental.
- **Loot mods that follow your level do not stall the server.** A mod that recomputes the loot tables while you play (the Kiln version of Loot Rarity Leveler does it at every level-up) has Kiln prepare the new tables beside the game and swap them in at once: a few milliseconds on the server in our tests, where the first version of this held it for up to 2 seconds.
- **The launcher's Conflicts page names more writers.** It now names the ShroudForge or EML package behind each data change, also for changes a ShroudForge mod makes while the game runs on Kiln's own ShroudForge interpreter, and shows the hosted ShroudForge's change journal.
- **ShroudForge and EML mods after a game update (opt-in).** When a game update breaks a ShroudForge or EML mod's data edits, Kiln can put back that mod's last working effect through its registries until the author updates it. Kiln records every such mod's effect; it replays only the mods you switch on, one by one, on the Conflicts page (Effect replay) or with `kilnctl registry replay`. The first start after a ShroudForge or EML mod or the game changed takes a few seconds longer while Kiln records.
- **Gear mods show in the Conflicts page too.** Equipment rules mods set (the `gearRules` section) now go through Kiln's registry where Kiln has proven the result is the same, so they show who changed what and carry over game updates. Nothing changes in the game.

## Modders

- **Mod API 0.2.27 (was 0.2.26).** Everything 0.2.26 shipped is still there; the MDK packs are 0.2.0-dev.32. The new surface, in short (the full list is the dev.32 row of the skill's `versions.md`, the pages are in its `docs-map.md` under "dev.32+"):
  - **Registries:** `creatures`, `arsenals`, `perks`, `skills`, `stations`, `buffs`, `abilities`, `world`, `difficulty`, `limits`, `lootRules`, `randomLoot` (experimental, KILNX031); `RegistryViews`, one read-only view over every registry and resource type; `Registries.Reauthor()`, the `WorldLoad` pass again, prepared off the world tick and landed in one step; an edit cap per call (`randomLoot` 16,384, the others 4,096: a call over it is refused whole, never thrown); `RegistryStage.Entries(name)`, `RegistryContext.Pass` / `OffTick`, `creatures.tamingSteps`. Analyzer hints (KILN124) cover every new registry. Pages: `docs/kiln/modding/registries/README.md`, `views.md` and one page per registry.
  - **Kiln.Pets** and **Kiln.Taming** (new): `Pets`, `PetDefinition` and its policies, `PetBlock`, `ReviveBlock`; `Taming`, `TamingDefinition`, `FeedStep` / `WeakenStep` / `UseToolStep` / `NativeStep`, `TamingBlock`, `NativeTaming`. Pages: `modding/pets.md`, `modding/taming.md`; skill `api-pets.md`, `api-taming.md`.
  - **NPC dialogue:** `Npcs.Farewells` and `MenuLine` (as `NpcLines`), `MenuSay`, `Topics`, `SpeechPick`, `NpcTopic`, `NpcLineContext`, channel `kiln:npcdialog` beside v1. Page `modding/npc-dialogue.md`. Requested by Marshall (Ryrin) for Living Larder.
  - **Exact costs** (`ItemCosts`, `ItemSelection`, `CostReceipt`, `ItemCostSettledEvent`; `modding/item-costs.md`) and **checked moves** (`Items.MoveChecked`, `Items.CheckedMovesAvailable`, a KILN121 guard: a move that happens only while the slot still holds what your read saw; asked for by Flamebound Ledger); `ItemsResult.Ok` / `Reason`.
  - Effect replay and the hosted journal (formats only), baked terrain tiles (`kfc.py terrain`, tools only, `modding/terrain-tiles.md`), manifest `"native": true` (`modding/tiers.md`); experimental `LabBots` (Kiln.Players; lab servers only, it refuses anywhere else) and `NativeTaming.PetStep`; TestKit `KilnHarness.Pets`, `KilnHarness.Taming`, the NPC dialogue parts of `KilnHarness.NpcSpeech`, `HarnessRegistry.Refused` and `RunReauthored`; native `kiln_registry_view.h`, `kiln_pets.h`, `kiln_item_costs.h`, tail members in `kiln_npcspeech.h`, `kiln_assets.h` and `kiln_content.h`, `KILN_CAP_OWNER_MAX` 1024.
- **`RegistryEntry.Get<T>` now throws for a missing value.** In 0.2.26 it returned `default` (0, false) when the entry had no value of the field (a creature without the component the field lives in). Now a non-nullable `T` throws `InvalidOperationException` naming the field; read it as a nullable type (`int?`, `RegistryId?`, `string`), which gives null, or ask with the new `RegistryEntry.TryGet<T>(field, out value)`. The registry API is experimental (KILNX031), which is why this could change.
- **Renamed and replaced (all still build):** the entry class is `Registries` (`Registry` is obsolete and keeps working); `ItemLootEntry.Rarity` replaces `FixedRarity` and `ItemLootRarity` (obsolete). Dev builds of the Kiln version of Loot Rarity Leveler that called `Registry.Reauthor(point)` must call `Registries.Reauthor()`: the point form never shipped and is gone. The generated creature kind is `RegistryCreatureKind` and an arsenal's attacks are `RegistryArsenalAttack` (not `CreatureKind` / `ArsenalAttack`, which are Kiln.Creatures' names).
- **Fixes inside the new features since the lanes:** a `Registries.Reauthor()` pass that edits abilities or buffs keeps the events in order and Kiln's buff copy current; `registry.json` after a re-author names ShroudForge run-time writers; effect replay no longer refuses a package that only edits loot stack sizes or rarity weights; the views show randomLoot's "covered by" links.
- **The C# snippets of the dev.32 pages compile** with the Kiln analyzers and warnings as errors (a new check), so every example asks its capability row and checks its result.

## Not yet seen in a real game

This beta passed its host tests, the Windows test runs and five dedicated-server labs (registry views, the Loot Rarity Leveler lab, and pets, NPC dialogue and taming with a bot player). Not seen in a real game:
- Registries: creature, attack and perk edits in play, a joined player's skill screen, buff and ability timing, more than 16 altars, gifting past 50 m, cleared no-build zones, real loot rolls; a re-author's landing on abilities and buffs.
- Views on a game client, the analyzer hints in an editor, a `"native": true` mod end to end.
- Gear rules on a game client, a join with a 0.2.0-dev.31 player, tooltips and durability.
- Effect replay after a real game update, with joined players; the hosted ShroudForge journal on a player's PC; the Conflicts page with real packages.
- NPC dialogue: the first menu before the host answers, the per-player budget, a farewell on a real NPC.
- Pets: the bar, markers and HUD, animations, attack orders longer than 30 seconds, coming back at login, rough terrain.
- Taming: the game's own bait, eat and pet flow, who gets the credit, progress on a client, refunds.
- Loot re-authors during a hot reload or while drop tables are prepared, in co-op and hosted worlds.
- Baked terrain tiles read by the game.
